# COMPLIANCE GUIDELINE: RESTRICTED CURRENCIES & FX CONTROLS
**Region Focus:** LATAM (Brazil) & APAC (China)
**Last Regulatory Update:** May 28, 2025 (Brazil Decree 12.466), July 25, 2025 (China SAFE Notice)

## 1. Brazil (BRL) - Foreign Exchange & Tax Controls
The Brazilian Real is a non-convertible currency. Offshore BRL accounts are restricted. FX must be closed onshore.

### A. IOF Tax (Tax on Financial Operations) - 2025 Updates
**WARNING:** Per Federal Decree 12.466 (May 2025), tax rates on FX inflows have surged.
- **Inbound FX (Intercompany Loans):** 0% (only if minimum tenor > 180 days).
- **Inbound FX (Short-term/Cash Pooling):** **3.5%** (Increased from 1.1% to discourage "hot money").
- **Outbound Royalty Remittance:** 3.5% IOF + 15% WHT (Withholding Tax).
- **Payment for Services:** 3.5% IOF.
- **Equity Capital Injection:** 0.38% IOF (Must be registered via RDE-IED).

### B. Documentation & Reporting (RDE-ROF)
- **Threshold:** Inflows > USD 1,000,000 equivalent require prior registration in the Central Bank (BACEN) system.
- **System:** RDE-ROF (Electronic Declaratory Registration - Financial Operations).
- **Penalty:** Failure to link the FX contract to a valid RDE-ROF results in a fine of up to 5% of the transaction value.

## 2. China (CNY/CNH) - SAFE Cross-Border Pooling (Ver 3.0)
**Policy Ref:** PBOC/SAFE Notice on Centralized Operation (July 2025).

### A. Cross-Border Sweeping Quotas
- **Outflow Cap:** Limited to 100% of the Owner's Equity in the domestic Master Account.
- **Inflow Cap:** Limited to 100% of the agreed foreign debt quota (Macro-prudential limit).
- **Currency:** Can settle in CNY or Foreign Currency (FCY).
- **SAFE Registration:** The Cash Pool Header must file a "Record Filing" with the local SAFE branch annually.

### B. "Purpose of Payment" Codes
- All CNY cross-border payments must carry a valid BOP (Balance of Payments) code.
  - `121010`: General Trade (Goods)
  - `222024`: Software Services
  - `822030`: Capital Injection
- **Prohibited:** Usage of Virtual Accounts (VA) for cross-border CNY without underlying trade background.

## 3. Sanctions & AML Blocks
- **Auto-Reject List:** North Korea, Iran, Syria, Cuba, Crimea Region.
- **PDVSA Rule:** Any transaction referencing "Petróleos de Venezuela" or its subsidiaries is blocked globally under OFAC GL 44A.
